A roll button is added to D&D Beyond's character sheet sidebars (abilities, skill checks, saving throws, weapon attacks, spells, class features, and more) that lets you either roll the dice directly into the VTT chat, or display the spell card, feature or equipment item for others to see. It also has an integrated dice roller for those who do not use a VTT, and rolls can also be sent to Discord.

Head over to the [Screenshots](screenshots) page to see it in action.

## What this fork adds

This is a **personal, unofficial fork** of [Beyond20](https://github.com/kakaroto/Beyond20). Everything Beyond20 normally does still works; the additions are on top of it. It is based on Beyond20 v2.21.0.

### Roll20 Quick Roll Launcher

Roll a skill, saving throw or ability check from the Roll20 page, without switching tabs.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pick a roll** | A floating button on the Roll20 page shows your D&D Beyond character's portrait. Click it to open a panel of skills, saving throws and ability checks. |
| **Roll it for real** | Click a roll and it is performed on your real D&D Beyond character sheet, so class features, effects and custom modifiers apply exactly as if you had clicked the sheet yourself. The result appears in the Roll20 chat as usual. |
| **Stay put** | Your D&D Beyond tab stays in the background and is never brought to the front. |
| **Never guess the sheet** | It remembers which character you play in each Roll20 game, and asks rather than guessing when it cannot tell. Several sheets open at once can be switched between from the panel header. |
| **Make it yours** | Light and dark themes, and a choice of colour for the panel's text and highlights. |
| **Or turn it off** | The launcher can be disabled in the settings, under the Roll20 tab's options. |

See the [Features](features) page for the full feature list. This fork is **not** published to the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons, so it is installed from source — see [Install](install).
