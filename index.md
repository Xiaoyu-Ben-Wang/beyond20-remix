[![Cast on VTT](images/cast-on-vtt.png)](install)

A roll button appears on the side panels of the D&D Beyond character sheet. It covers abilities, skill checks, saving throws, weapon attacks, spells, class features and more. Click it to roll the dice into your VTT chat. You can also show the spell card, feature or item to other players instead. The extension has its own dice roller for people who do not use a VTT. You can send rolls to Discord as well.

Look at the [Screenshots](screenshots) page to see it working.

## What this fork adds

This is a personal, unofficial fork of [Beyond20](https://github.com/kakaroto/Beyond20). It is based on Beyond20 v2.21.0. Everything Beyond20 normally does still works. This fork adds one thing on top.

It adds a **Roll20 quick roll launcher**. A floating button appears on the Roll20 page. The button shows your D&D Beyond character's portrait. Click it to open a panel of skills, saving throws and ability checks. Click a roll to make it. The roll happens on your real character sheet, so class features, effects and custom modifiers all apply. The result appears in the Roll20 chat as usual. Your D&D Beyond tab stays in the background. The launcher remembers which character you play in each Roll20 game. It has light and dark themes, and a choice of accent colour. You can turn it off in the settings.

**[Try the demo](demo)** — it is a working simulation of the launcher. You do not need the extension.

This fork is **not** in the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons. You install it from source — see [Install](install).
